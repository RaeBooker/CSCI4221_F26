Currently I been working on the features and so far what I have is
Input checks and useful feedback
Before displaying a result, the application should check the input. A decimal input must be a whole number between 1 and 3999. Roman inputs must follow standard Roman numeral ordering and subtraction rules. The app should reject inputs such as `0`, `4000`, `VX`, or `IIII` and explain what the user should correct. Lowercase letters may be accepted by normalizing them to uppercase. Correct error handling is important because showing a believable but incorrect answer would defeat the purpose of the calculator.

I've also gotten my persona which you could see in my Pbi table and her name is Alexis
Alexis is a first-year college student and  is 18 and needs to translate Roman numerals for a class assignment. She sometimes remembers the symbols but struggles with subtraction combinations such as `IX`, `XL`, and `XC`. She usually checks answers from her phone between classes. Her main needs are quick conversions, minimal steps, and buttons that do not become cramped on a small screen.
